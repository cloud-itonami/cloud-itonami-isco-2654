# Business Model: Independent Video Production Studio

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-2654`
- ISCO-08: `2654`
- Occupation: Film, Stage and Related Directors and Producers
- Social impact: creative-livelihoods, cultural-expression, local-jobs

## Customer

- small businesses and NPOs needing video content
- channels with recurring publication cadence
- event organizers (stage, live capture)
- agencies subcontracting production

## Offer

- video planning and direction
- shooting (robot camera operation, gated)
- editing and assembly (timeline → render plan → delivery)
- distribution packages and channel operations

## Revenue

- per-production fee
- monthly production retainer
- distribution revenue share

## Trust Controls

- no publication without subject consent and rights clearance
- location/set safety constraints require human sign-off to override
- BGM and asset licensing is verified before render (render-only policy)
- timeline, rights and delivery records are auditable, not editable
